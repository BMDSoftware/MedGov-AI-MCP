Structure:
- ST000000 = Study (one patient scan session)
- SE000000 = Series 0 (5 slices) - probably a localizer/scout
- SE000001 = Series 1 (27 slices) - an MRI sequence
- SE000002 = Series 2 (32 slices) - another MRI sequence
- thumbs/ = PNG thumbnail previews (generated by a DICOM viewer, not useful)
- dicom/ = duplicate reference folder with a UID-named subfolder (viewer metadata)
- ctkDICOM.sql / ctkDICOMTagCache.sql = database files from 3D Slicer or CTK viewer
